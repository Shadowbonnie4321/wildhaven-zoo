Frequently Asked Questions

What is Wildhaven Zoo?

Wildhaven Zoo is a creative zoo project focused on designing animal habitats, sharing animal facts and documenting the building process.

What does “Wildlife Without Limits” mean?

It's our slogan: a celebration of wildlife, discovery and the endless variety of the natural world.

Can I watch the zoo being built?

Yes! Visit the YouTube section to watch the Asian Elephant habitat-building episode and future project videos.

Will more animals be added?

Absolutely! The animal directory and habitat pages can expand as the project grows.

Is this a real-world zoo?

This website presents the Wildhaven Zoo creative project. Details about habitats and residents relate to the project, rather than claiming real-world zoo facilities.
